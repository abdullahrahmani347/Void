Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 03, 2026

VOID Streaming is a demo catalog interface for browsing movie and TV metadata. This page explains what little data the app handles.

Data we store

All personal data — profiles, watchlist, diary entries, reviews, settings and PIN hashes — lives only in your browser's local storage on your own device. It never reaches our servers, and clearing your browser data removes it permanently.

Third-party services

Movie and TV metadata and artwork are provided by TMDB. Playback uses third-party embedded players, which are subject to their own providers' policies. Fonts are served by Google Fonts.

Tracking

VOID ships no analytics, no ads and no tracking pixels. The error console records failures locally in memory only and is never transmitted.

Children

VOID is a general-audience metadata browser. Profiles support a maturity toggle, but no age verification is performed.

Changes

Changes to this policy will be published on this page with an updated date.
